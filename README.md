## 📊 Demographics & Methodology
*   **Target Audience:** UoM undergraduates and regular users of university shared spaces.
*   **Methodology:** Anonymous digital questionnaire prioritizing user privacy (no personally identifiable information collected).
*   **Total Responses:** 37

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. The Problem: Overcrowded Spaces

![Frequency of Space Usage]

*   **Space Usage:** 67.6% of respondents use university libraries, or study areas on a Daily basis, while another 27% use them several times a week. This proves our target areas see highly consistent, heavy foot traffic.
*   **Search Friction:** A significant majority indicated they "Frequently" or "Sometimes" have difficulty finding an available seat in these public areas.
*   **Demand for Solution:** When asked how useful it would be to know seat availability before entering a crowded area, the overwhelming majority rated it a 4 or 5 (Very useful).
